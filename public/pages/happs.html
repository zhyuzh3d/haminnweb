Chataxi

把喜欢的 AI，放在一起聊。Bring the AIs you like into one conversation.

GitHub 仓库GitHub repository

Chataxi 是一个装进 Haminn 的 AI 对话应用：连接自己的模型服务或局域网服务，创建角色，然后开始聊天。每个角色都有身份性格和行为指导两份设定——前者决定它是谁，后者决定它怎么说话、怎么把对话一步步推下去；再加上真人感的流式语音和真正的多角色群聊，所有对话记录都留在这台手机里。角色还能在对话里直接作画：它自己决定什么时候画、写什么提示词，图片就地挂在对话里，可以全屏放大、也可以翻看这一程画过的每一张。Chataxi is an AI chat happ for Haminn: connect your own model service or a local network endpoint, create roles, and start talking. Every role carries both an identity and its own behavior guidance — who it is, and how it speaks and pushes the scene forward — on top of lifelike streaming speech and real multi-role group chat. Conversations stay on this phone. Roles can also draw right inside the chat: they decide when to paint and what to ask for, the picture lands in the conversation, and you can open it full-screen or flip back through everything drawn so far.

HamDraw

随手涂鸦，实时成图。Doodle freely. Watch it become a picture.

GitHub 仓库GitHub repository

HamDraw 是第一款真正实时的 AI 绘图 happ。你在画布上随手涂几笔，成图层就跟着变成一张完整的图；再用「局部」涂红一块、写下这块要改成什么，就只改这一块。提示词和等待式的图生图都不够用——人需要的是一边说、一边画、一边看见。三个任务槽位各自配模型：快速生图、局部重绘、高清渲染；推荐在本机 ComfyUI 装官方插件（CHP，密码在插件配置节点里设置），也可以接云端服务。HamDraw is the first genuinely real-time AI drawing happ. Scribble a few strokes and the result layer turns them into a finished picture; paint a region with Mask, describe what it should become, and only that region changes. Prompts alone are not enough, and waiting for image-to-image is not enough either: you need to talk, draw and see at the same time. Three task slots take their own models — quick draw, local redraw and render — best served by the official plugin (CHP) on your own ComfyUI, where the password lives in the plugin's config node.

PoseGi

先把姿势摆对,再让 AI 画。Pose it right first, then let the AI draw.

GitHub 仓库GitHub repository

PoseGi 是一个装进 Haminn 的 3D 摆姿应用:在手机上拖动一根人形骨架摆出造型,再让 AI 照着画出来。骨架有 20 多个解剖学关节,每个关节只在自己的可转范围内活动;自己手写的 IK 会把你的拖动顺着骨链传下去,所以拖手拖脚的时候整条链跟着走。摆好之后手机现场渲染一张 1024 边的参考图,连同你写的画面描述交给模型卡 —— 本地 ComfyUI 插件,OpenAI Images 兼容接口,SD WebUI 或者 Stability AI 都能接。角色,姿态和生图配置全部留在本机,不内置任何平台密钥。PoseGi is a 3D posing happ for Haminn: drag a humanoid skeleton on your phone and let the AI draw from it. The rig carries more than twenty anatomical joints, each moving only inside its own range, and a hand-written IK carries your drag down the bone chain so the whole limb follows. When the pose is right the phone renders a 1024-edge reference on the spot and hands it to a model card along with your description — a local ComfyUI plugin, an OpenAI Images compatible endpoint, SD WebUI or Stability AI all connect. Figures, poses and generation settings stay on this phone, and no platform key is bundled.
