第一件：语音One: the voice

文字还没写完，
声音已经在说。The voice starts
before the text ends.

不是把整段话快点读完。模型每输出一段，立刻送进语音合成；音频分片一到就排队播放——大约三秒缓冲之后开口，剩下的边说边到。Streaming here is not faster reading of a finished paragraph. Every segment the model emits goes straight into speech synthesis, and each audio fragment queues for playback the moment it lands: talking starts after roughly three seconds of buffer, and the rest arrives while it speaks.

  1. 模型按原生协议逐段输出，字幕同步逐段出现。The model streams on its native protocol and the transcript fills in as it arrives.
  2. 每段直接进入 ElevenLabs 的流式或对话式接口，也支持 OpenAI、豆包的流式语音。Segments go into an ElevenLabs streaming or dialogue socket, or a streaming OpenAI or Doubao endpoint.
  3. 音频分片边到边播。流式通道在首个分片前失败，自动回退完整音频。Fragments play as they arrive. If a streaming channel fails before its first fragment, it falls back to full audio.

离开应用自动暂停，回来接着播；说到一半切静音不会打断这一句。不接第三方服务也能用系统朗读兜底。Leaving the app pauses playback and returning resumes it; muting mid-sentence does not cut the sentence off. System speech covers you with no third-party service at all.

第二件：群聊Two: the group

一屋子角色，
不是一个模型自言自语。A room full of roles,
not one model talking to itself.

同一个模型自己和自己讨论，只会不断同意你。真正的对话里有立场、有反驳、有分工——那需要一个房间里不止一个人。A single model talking to itself mostly agrees with you. Useful conversations contain positions, rebuttals and division of labour, which needs more than one participant in the room.

一桌人同场Everyone is present

每个角色都拿到全部角色设定、个人设定和共享上下文，不是各自开小窗。别人刚说的话，下一位真的看得见。Every participant receives the full role list, the personal setting and the shared context. The next speaker genuinely sees what was just said.

主持人调度A host routes the room

先由你选定的主持人按完整上下文决定谁接话，那个角色再正式回复。预选不写进消息历史，不会污染对话。Your chosen host reads the whole context, names the next speaker, and only then does that role reply. The routing step never enters the message history.

角色是独立个体Roles are individuals

每个角色可以绑定自己的模型、温度、推理强度、音色和发音参数，还各有「身份性格」和「行为指导」两份设定：一个决定它是谁，一个决定它怎么说话、怎么把对话推下去。写代码的用强推理模型，陪聊的用低延迟无推理模型。A role can bind its own model, temperature, reasoning effort, voice and pronunciation settings, and carries two settings of its own: an identity that says who it is, and behavior guidance that says how it speaks and pushes the scene forward. Use a heavy reasoning model for code and a low-latency one for company.

功能细节Details that matter

该较真的地方，都较真了。The details hold up to a closer look.

对话层的场景与个人设定Scene and profile per conversation

每个对话有自己的名称、介绍和头像，空字段才跟随“我的”。头像走受控的选取与裁切，拖动、捏合或加减都行。Name, bio and portrait are stored per conversation and fall back to your profile only when empty. Portraits go through a controlled picker and cropper, with drag, pinch or buttons.

每个角色一条音色One voice per role

同一个对话里给每个角色配不同音色与发音参数，一耳朵分得清谁在说话，也能用服务商提供的声音复刻资源。Give every role its own voice and pronunciation settings inside one conversation, so you hear who is talking, using your provider's cloned voices if you have them.

身份性格与行为指导分开写Identity and behaviour are separate

每个角色有两份设定：「身份性格」说它是谁，「行为指导」说它怎么说话、怎么把对话一步步推下去——不抢话、不替对方下结论、该反问就反问。27 个模板都自带行为指导，主持人挑谁接话时也会读它。Each role holds two settings: identity says who it is, behavior guidance says how it speaks and how it pushes the scene forward. All 27 templates ship with their own guidance, and the host reads it when choosing who speaks next.

编辑和重新生成分开Edit and regenerate are separate

编辑只保存你改的文字，不顺手生成、也不删历史。重新生成独立执行，后面还有消息时必须先确认。Editing saves only the text you changed and never generates or deletes history. Regeneration is its own action and asks first when later messages exist.

图片也进对话Images join the conversation

对话里可以直接发图当参考，支持图片的模型就会看见它，和角色们一起讨论。Send an image into the conversation as reference; models that accept images will see it and discuss it along with everyone else.

场景开场白A generated opening

闲聊、思辨、学习、工作、倾诉五种模式各有独立策划提示，主持人生成约一百字的开场白，落成一条可编辑的普通消息。Five modes - small talk, debate, study, work, confiding - each with its own brief, and the host writes a short opening that becomes an ordinary editable message.

说也行，打也行Talk or type

系统语音识别边听边出字，录音时能看到真实麦克风波形；也可以接第三方语音输入。System recognition fills the text in as you speak and the waveform shows the real microphone level; a third-party voice input can be connected instead.

自动压缩，看得懂Compression you can reason about

保留多少条最近消息不用逐对话去调：每轮从最新一条往前累加字数，刚超过设置里「压缩保留字数」（默认 4000）时的条数就是保留窗口，至少两条，保证最新一问一答始终以原文参与。累计超过「触发字数」时，主持人角色在自己的模型上于后台压缩更早的消息，压缩期间照常回复、不打断本轮。How many recent messages survive compression is not a per-conversation setting: each turn counts backwards from the newest message until it passes the keep-word budget (4000 by default), and that count is the window, never fewer than two, so the latest exchange always stays verbatim. Past the trigger budget the host compresses older messages on its own model in the background while the conversation carries on.

数据留在这台手机Data stays on this phone

对话、角色和配置都保存在 Chataxi 自己的数据区，不上传服务器，也不需要注册账号。Conversations, roles and settings live in Chataxi's own data partition. Nothing is uploaded and there is no account to create.
